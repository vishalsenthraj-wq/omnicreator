These terms govern use of OmniCreator AI ("the app"), a comment-triage tool for a single connected Instagram Business Account. By connecting an Instagram account and using the app, the account owner agrees to these terms.

What the app does

  • Reads recent posts and public comments on the connected Instagram Business Account via the Instagram Graph API.
  • Classifies each new comment (fan praise, business collaboration inquiry, resource request, or spam) using Google's Gemini AI.
  • Drafts a suggested reply for non-spam comments.
  • Sends a push notification to the account owner when a comment looks like a business or sponsorship opportunity.
  • Posts a reply back to Instagram only after the account owner reviews and explicitly approves it through the app's dashboard.

Human control

The app does not post, delete, or modify any content on Instagram without the account owner's explicit, per-comment approval. The account owner may edit any suggested reply before approving it, or reject it so nothing is posted.
